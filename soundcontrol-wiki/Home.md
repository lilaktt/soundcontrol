## ⌨️ Quick Tips

- **Don't know the sound name?** Press `Y` in-game to enable the 3D Sound Radar — it shows sound IDs floating at their source in the world.
- **Basic Mode** groups sounds (e.g., `minecraft:entity.zombie` covers all zombie sounds).
- **Advanced Mode** lets you control each individual sound.
- **Mods Mode** shows sounds from installed mods in a sidebar.
